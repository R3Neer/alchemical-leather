# Temporary architecture plan: dyeable armor infusions

Status: temporary working document. Delete before merging to `main`.

## Current-system analysis

Alchemical Leather currently has four tightly coupled assumptions:

1. The released alpha recognizes only the four vanilla humanoid leather pieces.
2. Humanoid effects are mapped to HEAD/CHEST/LEGS/FEET and multi-effect potions are rejected there.
3. The released runtime does not inspect `EquipmentSlot.BODY`.
4. Colored-water cauldron dyeing currently exists only through optional BedrockIfy compatibility, so Alchemical Leather is not self-sufficient for the full dye/infuse/wash loop.

The branch under development has already generalized armor classification to standard dyeable armor, added BODY support, preserved humanoid slot restrictions, and added a one-potion animal infusion bundle. This revision extends that architecture so the complete colored-water mechanic belongs to Alchemical Leather itself rather than depending on BedrockIfy.

## Requested behavior

- Compatibility is based on **dyeability, not leather material**.
- Any dyeable item whose `DataComponents.EQUIPPABLE` slot is an armor slot is eligible, including mod-added dyeable armor discovered without per-item special cases when it uses standard Minecraft dyeing semantics.
- Dyeable humanoid armor in HEAD/CHEST/LEGS/FEET keeps the effect-to-body-part restrictions and the existing one-effect infusion format. Multi-effect potions remain rejected for humanoid armor.
- Dyeable animal armor in BODY carries exactly **one potion infusion at a time**. Reinfusing replaces that potion; separate potions are never accumulated on one armor item.
- One BODY-armor potion may itself contain several effects, for example Turtle Master; all effects belonging to that one potion are retained together.
- BODY armor is not constrained by the humanoid effect-to-slot table: its one stored potion may be any effectful potion.
- Normal and splash potion effects retain their individual durations and pause while the armor is not equipped.
- Lingering potion effects remain stable/infinite while equipped.
- Instant effects fire once when the infused armor is equipped and are then consumed from that potion infusion; non-instant sibling effects from the same potion remain.
- Existing enchantment mutual exclusion, serialization and external-effect ownership semantics remain intact.
- **Alchemical Leather itself owns a complete dyed-water cauldron mechanic**: dye can be added to ordinary water, colors can be mixed, dyeable armor can be colored from the cauldron, and ordinary water removes color/infusion. BedrockIfy is optional compatibility, not a feature dependency.

## Dyeability and armor classification

Use a server-data reload index of items that are dyeable under the standard Minecraft item-color recipe system:

- scan loaded `data/*/recipe/**/*.json` resources during server-data reload;
- collect targets of recipes whose `type` is `minecraft:crafting_dye`;
- support the vanilla ingredient encodings accepted by `Ingredient`: a single item ID, a `#tag` target, or a homogeneous list of item IDs;
- cache direct item IDs and target item tags rather than scanning recipes during gameplay.

An item is Alchemical-Leather-compatible when:

1. it has `DataComponents.EQUIPPABLE`;
2. `equippable.slot().isArmor()` is true, covering both Minecraft 26.2 slot types `HUMANOID_ARMOR` and `ANIMAL_ARMOR`; and
3. it is known by the dyeability index or explicitly included in `#alchemical_leather:dyeable_armor`.

The fallback tag covers genuinely dyeable armor implemented through a non-standard custom dye mechanic. The primary automatic path remains standard `minecraft:crafting_dye` data.

`Infusions.slot(stack)` returns the actual qualifying armor slot. `Infusions.accepts(stack, actualSlot, effectId)` applies the policy split:

- `EquipmentSlot.Type.HUMANOID_ARMOR`: require `EffectSlotRules.slot(effectId) == actualSlot`;
- `EquipmentSlot.Type.ANIMAL_ARMOR`: accept every valid effect.

### Client-side prediction boundary

The exact dyeability index is server-data-derived and must remain server-authoritative. A remote client cannot be assumed to have the same static reload-listener cache.

Therefore cauldron interaction prediction uses a deliberately broader `armorCandidate(stack)` check: `EQUIPPABLE` exists and its slot `isArmor()`. On the server, all mutations still require the exact `Infusions.slot(stack)` classifier. This avoids multiplayer clients incorrectly returning `PASS` for valid datapack/mod dyeable armor while preventing client-side recipe data from becoming a second source of truth.

## Potion-resolution architecture

- `resolveAll(PotionContents, bottleItem)` accepts any effectful potion and converts every effect instance into timed/stable/instant `Infusion` entries. It preserves repeated effect IDs as separate timed sources.
- The legacy/single resolution path remains for humanoid armor and requires exactly one effect plus a registered humanoid slot rule.
- Potion cauldrons accept any effectful potion, because an effect unsupported by humanoid armor may be valid for BODY armor.
- Humanoid armor stores the legacy single component.
- Animal armor stores one `AnimalInfusion` bundle representing **one potion**, containing all effects supplied by that potion.
- Effectless contents such as mundane/thick potions remain non-infusible.

## Data model

Preserve `alchemical_leather:infusion` unchanged for backwards compatibility.

`alchemical_leather:animal_infusion` is a non-empty list of `Infusion` entries that semantically represents **one potion infusion**, not a collection of independently added potions. The list exists only because a single potion can contain multiple or repeated effect instances.

`Infusions.blocked` is true for either component. Tooltips show the effect(s) of the stored infusion. Washing removes both component types.

## Runtime synchronization

Watch HEAD/CHEST/LEGS/FEET/BODY.

- Humanoid dyeable armor uses the existing single-effect ledger path and slot policy.
- Animal dyeable armor projects all non-instant effects from its single potion bundle.
- Instant animal entries are removed before firing so they cannot replay after death/removal.
- Repeated entries of the same effect keep independent clocks; only their currently strongest source is projected.
- All equipped animal timed entries decrement independently after the effect tick.
- If a stronger repeated source expires, a surviving weaker source is reprojected immediately without a blank tick.
- Unequipping pauses item clocks and removes only armor-owned projected effects.
- `EffectLedger` remains the arbiter between armor-owned and external vanilla effects.

## Native dyed-water cauldron

Add an Alchemical Leather block `alchemical_leather:dyed_water_cauldron` with a synchronized block entity containing the current RGB color.

### Fluid-level model

Use six internal levels, matching BedrockIfy's dyed-water granularity and conversions:

- vanilla water level 1/2/3 converts to dyed-water level 2/4/6 when the first dye is added;
- applying color to one armor item consumes one dyed-water level;
- a glass bottle consumes two dyed-water levels and returns an ordinary water potion;
- a water bucket can be filled only from dyed-water level 6 and then empties the cauldron;
- adding a water potion to dyed-water level `n < 6` clears the tint and creates vanilla water level `min(floor(n / 2) + 1, 3)` while consuming the potion; at level 6 it is already full and the interaction succeeds without consuming the potion;
- pouring a water bucket into any non-full dyed-water cauldron clears the tint and produces a full vanilla water cauldron, following ordinary bucket fill semantics.

The dyed-water block behaves as a cauldron for comparator/content-height/clone/drop/tag purposes and drops/picks as an ordinary cauldron, just like the existing potion-cauldron illusion. Its six logical levels map to the three vanilla water-cauldron visual models as 1-2 → visual level 1, 3-4 → visual level 2, and 5-6 → full.

### Adding and mixing dye

A dye is recognized by `DataComponents.DYE`, not by a concrete `DyeItem` class, so modded standard dyes can participate.

- Using a dye on vanilla `WATER_CAULDRON` creates `alchemical_leather:dyed_water_cauldron` at level `waterLevel * 2` with the dye's diffuse RGB color.
- Using another dye on an Alchemical Leather dyed-water cauldron blends the existing water color with the new dye using the same brightness-preserving average semantics as `DyedItemColor.applyDyes` / BedrockIfy.
- If the blend produces the same RGB value, the interaction succeeds without consuming another dye.
- Otherwise one dye item is consumed in survival, the color is updated, and the fluid amount is unchanged.

### Dyeing armor from colored water

For every exact qualifying dyeable armor item, interaction with Alchemical Leather dyed water:

- blends the cauldron color with the armor's current `DYED_COLOR` if present; otherwise applies the cauldron color;
- preserves both infusion component types and every unrelated component;
- consumes exactly one dyed-water level;
- empties the cauldron when the last unit is consumed.

This deliberately matches the color-mixing semantics of normal Minecraft dyeing rather than blindly replacing the previous item color.

### Washing armor

For every exact qualifying dyeable armor item, vanilla water cauldrons:

- remove `DYED_COLOR`;
- remove `alchemical_leather:infusion` and `alchemical_leather:animal_infusion`;
- consume exactly one vanilla water level;
- also work when the item is merely dyed and not infused.

Thus every armor item that Alchemical Leather calls dyeable gets a complete and internally consistent dye/infuse/wash lifecycle.

## BedrockIfy coexistence

BedrockIfy remains optional and must not be required for any Alchemical Leather gameplay feature.

The two mods can coexist without disabling whole subsystems by assigning ownership per block/entry point:

1. **Alchemical Leather dyed-water block:** always owned and handled only by Alchemical Leather, whether BedrockIfy is installed or not.
2. **BedrockIfy colored-water/potion blocks:** owned by BedrockIfy. Alchemical Leather compatibility may intercept only its own armor-specific infusion/recolor behavior on those blocks, then returns success so BedrockIfy's own armor handler cannot also consume fluid.
3. **Vanilla water + dye:** Alchemical Leather yields only when BedrockIfy's cauldron feature is actually active, defined conservatively as both (a) the BedrockIfy colored-water block being registered and (b) reflective `Bedrockify.getInstance().settings.bedrockCauldron == true`. If BedrockIfy is absent, its cauldron feature is disabled, or reflection cannot confirm that the feature is active, Alchemical Leather owns this entry point and creates its native dyed-water block.
4. **Vanilla water + qualifying armor wash:** Alchemical Leather remains responsible for removing its own infusion components and color. Because this is an armor interaction, a successful callback terminates processing and avoids a second vanilla/BedrockIfy wash.

Compatibility with BedrockIfy's colored water must reproduce its actual semantics: blend with an item's existing color and decrement exactly one of its six internal levels. Potion-cauldron compatibility remains reflective and optional.

No shared block IDs, registry replacement, handler clearing, mixin priority games or feature-wide disable flags are needed. Alchemical Leather never mutates BedrockIfy's settings.

## Cauldron transaction rules

Every mutation stays atomic: validate target classification, potion/effect policy, enchantments and source state before consuming a dye, fluid dose or replacing an item stack.

- Potion infusion sets `DYED_COLOR` to potion color and preserves unrelated components.
- Reinfusion replaces the previous Alchemical Leather infusion rather than accumulating another potion.
- Native/BedrockIfy colored water blends item color without removing its infusion.
- Vanilla water removes color and both infusion formats.

## Compatibility and extensibility

No compile-time dependency on Clinging Reoriented, Alex's Mobs, BedrockIfy or third-party armor mods.

Clinging/Reorientation work on compatible BODY armor because ANIMAL_ARMOR has no effect-slot restriction. Standard dyeable modded humanoid and animal armor is discovered from recipe data. `#alchemical_leather:dyeable_armor` covers custom dye systems.

## Architectural invariants

- Existing alpha.1 humanoid infusion data remains decodable.
- Humanoid effect-slot restrictions do not loosen merely because additional dyeable armor items become eligible.
- Multi-effect potions remain invalid for humanoid armor.
- An animal armor contains at most one potion infusion; its multiple entries can only be effects from that one potion.
- ANIMAL_ARMOR never depends on humanoid effect-slot datapack mappings.
- Standard dyeable modded humanoid and animal armor is discovered without per-item integration code.
- Every accepted dyeable armor receives consistent potion-color, colored-water recolor and vanilla-water wash behavior.
- Alchemical Leather's dyed-water mechanic is fully usable without BedrockIfy.
- BedrockIfy merely being installed never disables Alchemical Leather's vanilla-water dye entry point; only a positively detected active BedrockIfy cauldron feature does.
- Installing/activating BedrockIfy never causes a single click to consume dye/fluid twice.
- Repeated custom potion effects do not overwrite or freeze one another's clocks.
- External potion/effect ownership semantics remain separated from armor clocks.
- No global entity or inventory scan is introduced.
