# Temporary architecture plan: dyeable armor infusions

Status: temporary working document. Delete before merging to `main`.

## Current-system analysis

Alchemical Leather currently has four tightly coupled assumptions:

1. `Infusions.slot(ItemStack)` recognizes only the four vanilla humanoid leather pieces.
2. `EffectSlotRules` maps effects only to HEAD/CHEST/LEGS/FEET and `Infusions.resolve` rejects any effect without such a mapping.
3. `EquipmentInfusions` and entity equipment listeners inspect only the four humanoid armor slots.
4. The cauldron path therefore cannot infuse or activate `EquipmentSlot.BODY` items and cannot automatically support modded dyeable armor, even though Minecraft 26.2 explicitly distinguishes humanoid and animal armor slot types and the effect ledger itself is entity-generic.

The cauldron already stores complete `PotionContents`, so it can preserve multi-effect potions without a block-entity format change. `EffectLedger` already manages arbitrary effect identifiers independently and can remain the single projected armor source per effect while multi-effect animal-potion clocks live on the item.

## Requested behavior

- Compatibility is based on **dyeability, not leather material**.
- Any dyeable item whose `DataComponents.EQUIPPABLE` slot is an armor slot is eligible for Alchemical Leather mechanics, including mod-added dyeable armor discovered without per-item special cases when it uses standard Minecraft dyeing semantics.
- Dyeable humanoid armor in HEAD/CHEST/LEGS/FEET keeps the existing effect-to-body-part restrictions and the existing one-effect infusion format. Multi-effect potions remain rejected for humanoid armor.
- Dyeable animal armor in BODY carries exactly **one potion infusion at a time**. Reinfusing replaces that potion; separate potions are never accumulated on one armor item.
- One animal-armor potion may itself contain several effects, for example Turtle Master; all effects belonging to that one potion are retained together.
- BODY armor is not constrained by the humanoid effect-to-slot table: its one stored potion may be any effectful potion.
- Normal and splash potion effects retain their individual durations and pause while the armor is not equipped.
- Lingering potion effects remain stable/infinite while equipped.
- Instant effects fire once when the infused armor is equipped and are then consumed from that potion infusion; non-instant sibling effects from the same potion remain.
- Existing enchantment mutual exclusion, serialization and external-effect ownership semantics remain intact.
- Every armor item accepted as dyeable must participate consistently in the color mechanics: potion infusion applies potion color, BedrockIfy colored-water cauldrons can recolor it without removing the infusion, and water cauldrons remove both Alchemical Leather infusion data and dye color while consuming one water level.

## Dyeability and armor classification

Introduce a server-data reload index of items that are dyeable under the standard Minecraft item-color recipe system:

- scan loaded `data/*/recipe/**/*.json` resources during server-data reload;
- collect targets of recipes whose `type` is `minecraft:crafting_dye`;
- support the vanilla ingredient encodings accepted by `Ingredient`: a single item ID, a `#tag` target, or a homogeneous list of item IDs;
- cache direct item IDs and target item tags rather than scanning recipes during gameplay.

An item is Alchemical-Leather-compatible when:

1. it has `DataComponents.EQUIPPABLE`;
2. `equippable.slot().isArmor()` is true, covering both Minecraft 26.2 slot types `HUMANOID_ARMOR` and `ANIMAL_ARMOR`; and
3. it is known by the dyeability index or explicitly included in the Alchemical Leather fallback tag.

Provide `#alchemical_leather:dyeable_armor` as a compatibility extension point for mods whose armor is genuinely dyeable but implements dyeing through a non-standard/custom mechanic that cannot be inferred from `minecraft:crafting_dye` data.

This intentionally keys off the actual dye recipe mechanic, not leather repair material, item ID or hard-coded equipment asset. Vanilla leather player armor, leather horse armor and wolf armor all use `minecraft:crafting_dye` and therefore qualify naturally. Non-armor dyeable items remain excluded because their equipment slot is not an armor slot.

`Infusions.slot(stack)` becomes the centralized classifier and returns the armor slot for any qualifying dyeable armor. `Infusions.accepts(stack, actualSlot, effectId)` then applies the policy split:

- `EquipmentSlot.Type.HUMANOID_ARMOR`: require `EffectSlotRules.slot(effectId) == actualSlot`;
- `EquipmentSlot.Type.ANIMAL_ARMOR`: accept every valid effect.

## Potion-resolution architecture

Split potion parsing from humanoid slot policy:

- `resolveAll(PotionContents, bottleItem)` validates that potion contents exist and contain at least one effect, then converts every effect instance to an `Infusion` payload using timed/stable/instant rules. It does not consult `EffectSlotRules` and preserves repeated effect IDs as separate timed sources.
- The legacy/single resolution path remains for humanoid armor and requires exactly one effect plus a registered humanoid slot rule.
- Filling a potion cauldron uses `resolveAll`, because a potion unsupported by humanoid armor may still be valid for BODY armor.
- Infusing humanoid armor stores the legacy single component.
- Infusing animal armor stores one `AnimalInfusion` bundle representing **one potion**, containing all effects supplied by that potion.

Water, mundane/thick or otherwise effectless contents remain non-infusible because they carry no effect to apply.

## Data model

Preserve the existing `alchemical_leather:infusion` component for humanoid items and backwards compatibility.

Add `alchemical_leather:animal_infusion`, represented by a record containing a non-empty list of `Infusion` values. Semantically the record is **one potion infusion**, not a bag into which independent potions can be appended. Its list exists only because one potion can contain multiple effect instances. The list may contain repeated effect IDs because custom `PotionContents` can legally do so.

`Infusions.blocked` is true for either component. Washing removes either component. Tooltip rendering supports both, listing the effects of the one stored animal potion. Humanoid items continue to use the old component unchanged.

## Runtime synchronization

Expand watched equipment slots to HEAD/CHEST/LEGS/FEET/BODY.

Refactor `EquipmentInfusions` so each equipped qualifying stack yields effect entries from its stored infusion:

- humanoid dyeable armor: one legacy effect;
- animal dyeable armor: all effect entries belonging to its one `animal_infusion` potion bundle.

For each equipped animal bundle:

1. Consume each instantaneous entry immediately before executing it, so death/removal during the effect cannot replay an already-fired instant source.
2. Group non-instant entries by effect identifier for projection only.
3. For repeated entries of the same effect, project the currently strongest source using amplifier then duration/infinite priority; all underlying timed sources still keep independent clocks.
4. Let `EffectLedger` arbitrate that projected armor winner against external vanilla effects exactly as it does today.
5. After the entity effect tick, decrement every equipped animal `timed` entry by one independently, remove expired entries, and immediately reproject surviving repeated sources so no blank tick appears. Stable entries do not decrement.

Humanoid armor continues using the existing ledger-backed clock writeback path.

`EffectLedger` itself should not need architectural changes.

## Cauldron color and transaction rules

Keep every cauldron mutation atomic: validate potion, target classification, enchantments and slot authorization before consuming a dose or replacing an item stack.

For every qualifying dyeable armor item:

- potion infusion sets `DataComponents.DYED_COLOR` to the potion color while preserving unrelated components;
- BedrockIfy colored-water cauldrons replace `DYED_COLOR` only and preserve whichever infusion component is present;
- water cauldrons remove `DYED_COLOR`, `alchemical_leather:infusion` and `alchemical_leather:animal_infusion`, consuming exactly one water level;
- these color/wash behaviors also work when the qualifying dyeable armor is not currently infused, matching the existing leather-armor behavior.

Animal reinfusion removes any legacy single infusion and **replaces** the previous animal potion bundle. It never merges two separate potions. Humanoid reinfusion symmetrically removes any animal bundle.

## Compatibility and extensibility

No compile-time dependency on Clinging Reoriented, Alex's Mobs or third-party armor mods. Effect identifiers remain runtime-resolved by the existing registry logic.

Clinging/Reorientation work on compatible BODY armor because animal armor has no effect-slot restriction, not because of special-case effect code.

The automatic compatibility boundary is standard `minecraft:crafting_dye` semantics plus an actual armor equipment slot. `#alchemical_leather:dyeable_armor` covers custom dye systems that cannot be inferred generically from server data.

## Architectural invariants

- Existing alpha.1 humanoid infusion data remains decodable.
- Humanoid effect-slot restrictions do not loosen merely because additional dyeable armor items become eligible.
- Multi-effect potions remain invalid for humanoid armor.
- An animal armor contains at most one potion infusion; its multiple entries can only be effects from that one potion.
- Animal BODY armor never depends on effect-slot datapack mappings.
- An item is not eligible merely for being leather, repairable with leather, colorable in its renderer, or occupying an armor slot; standard dye recipe semantics or the explicit fallback tag establish dyeability.
- Standard dyeable modded humanoid and animal armor is discovered from loaded recipes without per-item integration code.
- All accepted dyeable armor receives consistent potion-color, colored-cauldron recolor and water-cauldron wash behavior.
- Repeated custom potion effects do not overwrite or freeze one another's clocks.
- External potion/effect ownership semantics remain separated from armor clocks.
- No global entity or inventory scan is introduced.
